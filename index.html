Baklava

بقلاوةBaklava · pistachio

Forty layers. One crunch you can hear.

The recipe, almost

Forty sheets of filo, brushed one by one with warm ghee. A thick bed of green pistachio in the middle. It goes into the oven pale and comes out loud. Then cold orange-blossom syrup hits the hot tray, and that hiss is the whole secret.

  • Filo ×40
  • Ghee
  • Pistachio
  • Orange-blossom syrup
Mtabbak

مطبّقMtabbak · cheese or nuts

Folded like a letter from home.

The recipe, almost

Dough stretched by hand on an oiled counter until you could read a newspaper through it. Folded around soft akkawi cheese, baked until it shatters, then soaked in qatr syrup while it’s still singing. Cheese for dessert? Trust us.

  • Paper-thin dough
  • Akkawi cheese
  • Qatr syrup
  • Crushed pistachio
Caramel

لاتيه كراميل مثلّجIced caramel latte

Slow-falling espresso, salted caramel, a little hel.

The recipe, almost

House caramel cooked to deep amber and salted, a spoon of it at the bottom. Cold milk and ice, then a double shot poured on top so it sinks in clouds. A pinch of cardamom, the way it’s done back home.

  • Double espresso
  • Salted caramel
  • Cold milk
  • Hel

رؤيتناOur vision

This whole thing is a thank you.

To everyone who marched, posted, donated, boycotted, or simply kept Palestine in their heart through everything it has lived: shukran. Palbox is how we say it back, with coffee and something sweet.

في أي مكان

We show up anywhere

No shop and no lease to start. We pick a random corner of the city, hang the tatreez and the lights, and for one night it becomes a little piece of home.

مجتمع

Come for the baklava, stay for the people

Events, sufra dinners, music and stories. Palbox is a community first and a business second.

تبرّع

Every donation does two jobs

Donations are welcome. They help Palbox grow, and part of everything we raise goes to funds supporting Palestinians.

The promise we’re building toward

Kids under five eat free. Always.

Too many children in Palestine never get to turn five. So once the truck is rolling, every child under five who comes to Palbox eats for free.

الطريقThe road

From nothing to a truck.

  1. Now

    An idea and four recipes

    Tiramisu, baklava, mtabbak, a caramel latte, a name we’re still choosing, and a lot of love.

  2. Next

    A table on a street corner

    A folding table, a tatreez cloth and a thermos of coffee. Pop-ups in random spots around Barcelona, our first events, and a donation jar on the table.

  3. Then

    A cart on wheels

    Regular spots, weekend markets and Sufra Sundays. The community gets a place to find us.

  4. One day

    The Palbox truck

    A real kitchen on wheels, bigger events, and the promise kept: kids under five eat free.

سفرةSufra · the shared table

Wherever we park, there’s a seat for you.

The truck sells sweets, and it also sets a table. We’ll pick a spot, dress it in tatreez and olive wood, and turn it into a corner of Palestine in Barcelona that anyone can sit down at.

Sufra Sundays

A long table next to the truck. One Palestinian dish a week, cooked and shared with whoever shows up.

The tatreez wall

Embroidered panels as our decoration. Visitors add one cross-stitch each, and the pattern grows over the season.

Teta’s recipe box

Bring a family recipe. Once a month, one of them joins the menu with your teta’s name on it.

Coffee & stories

Evening sessions with music, poetry and conversations about Palestine. Bring a friend who’s never been.
